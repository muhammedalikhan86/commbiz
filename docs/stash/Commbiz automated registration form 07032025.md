### **Section 1 – File authorisation**

## **Note:** 

Manual Authorisation is the default state for CommBiz Automated for file authorisation where you will log into CommBiz and authorise payments after payment files are delivered from your ERP software. 

Automated Authorisation enables payment files to be imported, approved and released for processing without the need for further manual approval by your staff within CommBiz.  Please discuss with your relationship team and implementation manager for suitability of Automated Authorisation. 

File Authorisation is set at a service level. Hence the file authorisation selected will be applied to all payment methods. Please refer to Section 5 of CommBiz Terms and Conditions for more information on CommBiz Automated.
